About FKG LIVE Studios

Lagos' premier music production studio — building Africa's next generation of creators, one cohort at a time.

Our Mission

A Creative Ecosystem, Not Just a Studio

FKG LIVE Studios isn't just a recording studio — it's a creative ecosystem. Artists record here, producers create here, and through FKG LIVE Academy, the next generation learns here.

A student might start in music production, collaborate with cinematography students on a music video, work with graphic design students on the album art — and leave with a complete portfolio and real industry experience.

The Vision

Cross-Discipline Collaboration

That cross-discipline model — students building real, shippable work together instead of sitting in isolated classes — is what compounds into reputation.

Reputation is what attracts the next generation of creators without us having to chase them.

We're not just building a studio. We're building a creative community that produces industry-ready talent through real projects, real collaboration, and real mentorship.
